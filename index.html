Coastal living is our specialty.

Glass walls that open to the ocean air, deep wood soffits, stone and stucco that stand up to salt and sun. These are homes where the details carry the design, and where the builder has to get them right.

We bring the artistry of custom designs to life.

Your architect draws the house. We build it the way it was meant to be, with a team of craftsmen who take pride in the fine details an exceptional home needs. Brad spent ten years as a project manager before starting Smith Structures, and clients work with him directly.

  • Communication

    Owners and their design team hear from Brad directly, with expectations set before work starts and kept along the way.

What we build

A fully inclusive custom home builder based in San Clemente, working from plans through finished home.
