How we work

Three business principles run through every phase of development and construction.

  • Communication

    Owners and their design team hear from Brad directly, with expectations set early and kept along the way.

  • Organization

    Schedules, trades and selections run with a project manager’s discipline, so a detailed build stays calm.

  • Integrity

    Respect for the design, the budget, the neighbors and the coastline, throughout the build.
